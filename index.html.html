Who I Am

About Me

Assalam u Alaikum! I'm Naina Bangash — a UI/UX Designer and Graphic Designer with a passion for creativity, clean visuals, and continuous learning.

I enjoy designing interfaces and engaging visual content, and turning ideas into meaningful digital experiences. I am also learning web development so I can bring my designs to life.

Alongside my journey in technology, I am also an Alimah and have completed Dars-e-Nizami from Wafaq-ul-Madaris Al-Arabia. I strongly believe in balancing Deen and Dunya — growing professionally while staying connected to Islamic knowledge and values.

Currently focused on growing my skills in UI/UX and graphic design, while learning web development and freelancing through hands-on projects and consistent practice. I aspire to create impactful solutions where faith, knowledge, and innovation go hand in hand.
